## Changes

The hero now leads with “Sustainable development. Rooted in nature.” Supporting copy explains what Planet Green does: natural farming, green communities, farmland restoration and traditional rice cultivation. Lifestyle slogans have been replaced with concrete descriptions across Philosophy, Farm Natura, project introductions, Our Story, the gallery, the philosophy carousel, Collaborations and the footer. Project categories now identify the actual offering.

About, Philosophy, Communities and Collaborations are edited into clearer English grounded in these sources. The vision and mission are paraphrases, not invented quotes. `research/live-pages-original.json` preserves the previous normalized live-page content so it can be distinguished from revised editorial copy in `src/data/live-pages.json`.

The acreage and soil-revitalisation figures are presented as figures published by the project websites. They are not carbon, biodiversity or sustainability performance measurements. No claims of carbon neutrality, quantified environmental savings, investment returns, certification, medical benefits or additional awards were introduced.

Policy wording, event dates and historical 2023 event content remain as originally imported. Careers still uses only the published HR contact information. Current project facts are separately verified against each official project website, and individual details are reflected in project summaries and enquiry dialogs.
